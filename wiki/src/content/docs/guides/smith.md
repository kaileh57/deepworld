**perk:** forges work twice as fast. [milestones](/ref/disciplines/smith/)

## first day

build a [forge](/ref/blocks/forge/): eight bricks around a blast furnace. inputs go left, fuel at the bottom, output on the right.

- flux: rock salt + bone meal.
- bronze: 3 copper + 1 tin.
- steel: 2 iron + coal + flux.

10 forge jobs is **apprentice smith**.

## the middle

put a cauldron on a smithing table for a smithing bench. quench a tool with water, rendered fat (tough), brine (keen), lava (tempered) or ghast tear cordial (pristine). socket a resonant shard, lapis, diamond or quartz. 5 of those is **quencher**.

## capstone

mantle alloy, cobalt steel, then hellsteel at the hellforge. 64 forge jobs is **master smith**.
