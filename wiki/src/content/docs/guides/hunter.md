**perk:** deep creatures drop extra ingredients. [milestones](/ref/disciplines/hunter/)

## first day

kill 25 creatures below y 1000 for **deep hunter**. glow squid and drowned drop blind eel, striders drop lava fish, hoglins drop hoglin chop.

## the middle

take 3 trophies for **trophy hunter**. each [layer page](/ref/layers/shallow-caverns/) lists its mobs.

## capstone

kill a warden for **warden slayer**. hushed ii food keeps sculk and wardens from hearing you. see [sound](/mechanics/hazards/#sound).
