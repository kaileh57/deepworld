**perk:** +1 heat protection. [milestones](/ref/disciplines/delver/)

## first day

take a stone pickaxe down past y 1000 for **first descent**. granite in the [granite trenches](/ref/layers/granite-trenches/) needs copper, calcite below it needs iron.

## the middle

make a forge and get steel for blackstone and basalt. **below the heat line** is y 600. from there on you need [heat armor](/mechanics/heat/) or cooling food.

| rock | pickaxe |
| --- | --- |
| granite | copper |
| calcite | iron |
| blackstone, basalt | steel |
| gloomstone | diamond |
| mantle rock | mantle alloy |
| scorched rock | cobalt steel |

## capstone

**into the mantle** at y 150, then **breakthrough** at y 0. the first player below y 0 puts a title on everyone's screen, once per world. visit six layers on the way for **layer walker**.

plan each leg with the [expedition planner](/tools/expedition/).
