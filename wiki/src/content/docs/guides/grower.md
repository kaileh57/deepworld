**perk:** double harvests. [milestones](/ref/disciplines/grower/)

## first day

forage wild rice, chili, mint, tea, coffee, beans, oats and herbs on the surface. plant any forage ingredient on a block. it grows from sprout to mature. right-click a mature plant to harvest it. 25 harvests is **green thumb**.

surface crops only grow in their own biomes in daylight.

## the middle

breed animals for **breeder**. young inherit plump, rich milk, fast, hardy and calm, with small mutations. check them with the guild journal. rich-milk cows give rich milk.

## capstone

cave crops (truffle, glowcap, ash pepper, ember root, hush moss, forge fig) only grow at their own depth. harvest 20 for **deep farmer**.
