**perk:** dowsing and surveys reach twice as far. [milestones](/ref/disciplines/archivist/)

## first day

craft a guild journal (book and four gold nuggets) and an expedition logbook (leather and a book and quill). the logbook writes itself. the first journal page from a landmark chest is **first page**.

## the middle

a survey kit (compass, copper, paper) shows your layer, biome and known landmarks within 500 blocks. walk within 24 blocks of a landmark to find it. three is **cartographer**.

a dowsing rod (resonant shard and sticks) tunes to an ore you right-click and chimes as you face the nearest one.

## capstone

find all 12 pages for **the whole story**. there's one [landmark](/ref/landmarks/miners_camp/) kind per layer. read what you've found on [the journal](/ref/story/journal/).
