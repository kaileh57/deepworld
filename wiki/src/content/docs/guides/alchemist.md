**perk:** charges drop more, stills yield more. [milestones](/ref/disciplines/alchemist/)

## first day

saltpeter drops from tuff. a black powder charge is sulfur, charcoal, saltpeter and paper. right-click a block with it and run. 10 charges is **powder monkey**. charges make [cave-ins](/mechanics/shoring/) likely.

| charge | does |
| --- | --- |
| black powder | 3x3x3, half drops |
| shaped | 12-deep bore |
| mining | ore only |
| demolition | 5x5x3, no drops |

## the middle

brewing stands take sulfur as fuel. a still makes mint oil, ember extract, glowcap essence and tonics. the pot makes rendered fat and brine. 10 extracts or tonics is **distiller**.

## capstone

compost from spoiled food, sulfur fertilizer and growth tonic speed up crops. fertilize 20 for **fertile**.
