**perk:** dishes come out one quality higher. [milestones](/ref/disciplines/cook/)

## first day

craft a clay pot (five clay balls) and set it on a campfire. put in 2 or more ingredients, press cook, and [stir](/mechanics/quality/) when the stick glows. bread, potatoes and meat make a start. **line cook** is 10 dishes.

## the middle

add a mill, churn and mortar for flour, butter and salt. a brick oven or iron pot takes more heat. cook 15 different named dishes for **signature seeker**, and 50 for **head chef**. find recipes in the [recipe browser](/tools/recipes/) and trace ingredients in the [dish planner](/tools/dish-planner/).

## capstone

10 masterwork dishes is **gourmet**: everyone on the server gets a fourth meal slot. then serve a [tasting menu](/mechanics/meal-slots/#tasting-menu) with a signature ingredient from every layer for one more.
