**perk:** lifts run at 12 m/s. [milestones](/ref/disciplines/engineer/)

## first day

lay rail out from the deepline terminal at spawn. 200 rails is **track layer**. copper blocks under the rail double cart speed.

## the middle

craft [lift stations](/ref/blocks/lift_station/) (gold blocks, iron and redstone). place them at the same x and z on each floor, keep the shaft clear, and install a gold block of cable per 64 blocks. ride one 10 times for **lift operator**.

## capstone

1,000 rails is **line builder**. couple carts into trains with an iron chain, and route them with rail stations, ticket machines, switch boxes and signals. gold and cobalt steel beds reach 32 and 48 m/s. price a line with the [rail calculator](/tools/rail/), and read [rail and lifts](/mechanics/rail/).
