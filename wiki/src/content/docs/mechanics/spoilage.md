food in your inventory spoils while you're online. below y 600 it spoils 10 times faster.

- raw ingredients last about 40 minutes of play, dishes about an hour.
- dry goods like rice, beans, oats and flour don't spoil.
- preserved dishes and preserving ingredients (salt, rock salt, sulfur salt, brine, cheese, dried kelp) don't spoil.
- chests stop spoilage, not just the ice box.

## keeping food

| container | keeps |
| --- | --- |
| jar | hold a dish in the other hand and use the jar to seal it. never spoils |
| lunchbox | 4 slots, nothing inside spoils |
| ration pack | 8 slots, preserved food only |
| insulated hamper | 8 slots plus ice in the first. fresh while there's ice, one ice per in-game day |

- the tooltip says fresh, then stale.
- spoiled food is still edible but makes you sick. it also makes compost.

for a deep trip, pack preserved or jarred food. the [expedition planner](/tools/expedition/) does the counting.
