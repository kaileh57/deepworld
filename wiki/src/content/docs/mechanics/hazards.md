each hazard can be turned off in `config/deepworld.json`.

## heat

below y 600. see [heat](/mechanics/heat/).

## cave-ins

y 600 to 1000. see [shoring and cave-ins](/mechanics/shoring/).

## cold water

y 900 to 1000. swimming freezes you and drains hunger unless you wear any leather armor or ate something warming.

## firedamp

coal seams, y 800 to 900. some pockets hold gas. a torch, lantern, campfire or flint and steel there explodes. hold a safety lamp to check the air. a ventilation fan within 12 blocks clears it.

## echoes

y 700 to 800. mining calls monsters from 40 blocks away unless you're hushed (hushed food or wool-wrapped boots) or mining with an echo shard socketed in your tool.

## fumes

sulfur springs, y 600 to 700. nausea and poison unless a fan is near or you ate something bitter.

## magma tides

the lava sea rises and falls 5 blocks over two in-game days. the tide shows on the action bar.

## darkness

y 300 to 450. torches burn out after 5 minutes. amethyst lamps don't.

## sound

hushed ii food keeps sculk sensors and wardens from hearing you. an echo shard socketed in your tool hides the sound of mining.

## tremors

every 30 to 60 minutes a random layer with players in it shakes. your camera shakes for a few seconds, wide rooms with no supports may cave in, and a fresh ore seam appears in nearby rock.

## spoilage

food spoils 10 times faster below y 600. see [spoilage](/mechanics/spoilage/).
