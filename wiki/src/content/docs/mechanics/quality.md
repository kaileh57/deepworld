put 2 to 6 ingredients in a cooking station and press cook. when the stick glows, click stir. hits raise quality, misses lower it. take the dish out within 30 seconds or it burns.

quality goes burnt, plain, good, fine, masterwork. focus on [cook](/ref/disciplines/cook/) and every dish comes out one step higher.

## heat under the station

heated stations read the block below them.

| block below | heat |
| --- | --- |
| campfire | low |
| soul campfire | soul |
| lit furnace or coal block | medium |
| magma or lava | high |
| ancient debris or a lit hellforge | extreme |

each station has a top heat, so some dishes need an iron pot or a brick oven. the crock, drying rack and ice box need no heat.
