## rail

a cart's top speed comes from the block under the rail.

| bed | speed |
| --- | --- |
| anything else | 8 m/s |
| copper block | 16 m/s |
| gold block | 32 m/s |
| cobalt steel block | 48 m/s |

carts speed up on their own over those beds. only straight runs reach top speed, and curves can derail.

## trains

click a cart with an iron chain, then the cart that goes behind it. up to 6 carts. shears uncouple a cart. the lead cart sets the speed.

| block | does |
| --- | --- |
| rail station | named stop beside the track. modes: stop, unload, load, dispatch |
| ticket machine | pick a station and your cart or train is routed there |
| switch box | beside the middle rail of a t-junction. flips for carts bound to the stations you tick |
| rail signal | holds a cart while another cart is on the track ahead, up to the next signal |

4 iron ingots on the rusted lever of a deepline signal cabin turn it into a working switch box.

## lifts

place lift stations at the same x and z on different floors. click one and pick a floor. the shaft between them must be clear. lifts run at 8 m/s, 12 m/s with [engineer](/ref/disciplines/engineer/) focus.

- a new shaft has no cable. each gold block you install (the gold block button) lets it reach 64 blocks.
- there's one car per shaft. if it's on another floor, call it and wait.
- freight mode sends everything in a chest or barrel next to one station to the one next to the other.

## paths

paved blocks like gravel, cobble, packed mud, paths and stone bricks give speed i on foot and speed ii to horses. grass you walk over a lot turns into a path.

## the deepline

the terminal sits at spawn (0, 1212, 0). lines run east 250 blocks to a depot, west 450 to a worker camp, south 1000 and north 2000 to stations.

work out times and costs with the [rail calculator](/tools/rail/).
