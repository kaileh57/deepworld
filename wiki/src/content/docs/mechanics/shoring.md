between y 600 and y 1000, breaking blocks in a room 5 or more wide can drop the ceiling after a rumble. it won't happen if there are logs, fences, walls, bars, chains or stairs within 4 blocks.

- charges make a cave-in likely.
- [tremors](/mechanics/hazards/#tremors) can also bring down wide rooms with no supports.
- turn it off with `cave_ins` in `config/deepworld.json`.
