you have three meal slots and one drink slot. they show on the action bar. a meal's buffs last until its time runs out.

- eating a dish from the same family refreshes its slot instead of taking a new one.
- natural regeneration is off. fresh food heals you.
- when two meals give the same buff, the strongest one counts.

## buffs

| buff | does |
| --- | --- |
| hearty | tops up saturation |
| rich | absorption when you eat |
| fresh | slow healing |
| sweet | speed |
| warming | faster attacks, keeps cold water off |
| cooling | heat protection |
| glowing | night vision |
| briny | water breathing, dolphin's grace at ii |
| earthy | haste and luck |
| bitter | clears poison, keeps fumes off |
| hushed | keeps echoes off, sculk too at ii |

## more slots

- the cook milestone **gourmet** (10 masterwork dishes) gives everyone a fourth slot, once per server.
- the first tasting menu gives everyone a permanent extra slot.
- meeting the weekly goal gives everyone an extra slot for a week.

## feasts

a feast table takes up to 10 dishes. hit serve and everyone within 8 blocks gets one feast meal with every buff merged, lasting 25% longer, plus one absorption heart per 4 dishes.

## tasting menu

serve 10 dishes at a feast that between them use a signature ingredient from every layer. each [layer page](/tools/layers/) lists its signature ingredients.

## generic dishes

anything that isn't a known recipe still cooks into a generic dish, like hearty stew. browse the known ones in the [recipe browser](/tools/recipes/).
