heat starts below y 600. the meter sits on the action bar: five squares, blue for what you block, orange or red for what gets through.

## heat per layer

| layer | y | heat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [molten echoes](/ref/layers/molten-echoes/) | 450 to 600 | 1 |
| [gloomstone trenches](/ref/layers/gloomstone-trenches/) | 300 to 450 | 2 |
| [mantle crust](/ref/layers/mantle-crust/) | 150 to 300 | 3 |
| [the hellmouth](/ref/layers/the-hellmouth/) | 0 to 150 | 4 |
| [nether stratum](/ref/layers/nether-stratum/) | below 0 | 5 |

## protection

| source | protection |
| --- | --- |
| obsidian-inlaid armor | 0.5 a piece, 2 for the set |
| mantle alloy armor | 0.75 a piece, 3 for the set |
| cobalt armor | 1 a piece, 4 for the set |
| hellforged armor | 1.25 a piece, 5 for the set |
| fire resistance | 2 |
| a cooling meal | 1, or 2 at cooling iii |
| delver focus | 1 |

armor adds up across pieces and rounds down. fire resistance counts from a potion or a meal.

## stages

heat minus protection is your stage.

- 0: nothing.
- 1: hunger drains faster.
- 2: slowness too.
- 3: you take damage every two seconds.
- 4 and up: damage every second, and more of it.

try the [heat calculator](/tools/heat/).
